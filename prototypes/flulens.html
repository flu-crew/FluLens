Phred bands, painted along each read in place of the strand shade. Quality declines toward the 3′ end, so a read that is orange at one edge is normal; one that is orange under a variant is not. The lit read keeps its highlight.
Marks are whole CODONS whose translated residue disagrees with the reference protein, in this product's frame — so a read carrying only synonymous changes draws clean, and the marks that remain are the ones the rest of this app calls nonsynonymous. Coloured by the residue scheme. A read that does not span all three bases of a codon is not translated.
The stack is a sample; the numbers are not. Above the cap the reads are thinned to every n-th in coordinate order, and the ratio is stated in the gutter. Depth, allele counts and every figure in the panel come from the full pass over the segment, not from what is drawn.

warm = excess nonsynonymous · cool = excess synonymous · grey = neither. Ends saturate. Read with care. At a single codon in a single sample you usually see one difference or none, so πN − πS is mostly reporting WHICH kind was observed, not which exceeds the other. On this run have both terms non-zero — those are the only cells where the comparison is a comparison. The pattern across many codons is the signal; one cell is not.
